Also decided by this pick

  • Type: iOS text styles at their default Dynamic Type sizes (Body 17, Footnote 13, Large Title 34), weights 400 and 600 only. Deck desktop’s 14/12 scale is too small for a phone held at arm’s length.
  • Icons: drawn here with Deck’s Hugeicons. The native build can swap them for SF Symbols, which follow Dynamic Type and weight on their own; Hugeicons keep the desktop’s look.
  • Lines: no separators inside lists; groups are split by space and a background step, as on the desktop.
  • Terminal: fits the desktop pane’s columns to the screen (64 cols here, so text is small), pinch to zoom 50–200%. It never resizes the pane on the desktop.
